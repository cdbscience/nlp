🤖 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

What is this product?

This platform implements a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system that allows users to ask natural language questions and receive accurate answers based on their own documents.

Instead of relying only on a language model’s general knowledge, the system retrieves relevant document fragments and uses them as context to generate precise, grounded answers.

Key idea: Your data remains the source of truth. The AI answers only based on the uploaded documents.

How does it work?

  • An administrator uploads files (PDFs or documents) through the admin interface.
  • The system processes and splits the content into searchable document chunks.
  • Users can ask questions in natural language.
  • The most relevant document fragments are retrieved.
  • An AI model generates a contextualized answer based on those fragments.

Who is this for?

  • Internal knowledge bases
  • Technical documentation search
  • Legal or compliance document analysis
  • Academic and research material exploration
  • Enterprise AI assistants grounded on private data
